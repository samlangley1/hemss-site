A local tool for independent creators

YouTube publishing,
at your pace.

Discover music trends, prepare slowed and reverb videos, and organize a daily publishing schedule on your own YouTube channel.

hemss is the Google authorization name for the Dmiko / yt-slower project. It runs on the operator's computer and connects to YouTube only with the channel owner's permission.

From discovery to scheduled release

One connected workflow.

01 / Discover

Follow the momentum

Compare public video statistics and recent growth to identify music attracting attention.

02 / Prepare

Shape the final video

Process source audio with slowdown and reverb, render a background, and prepare relevant titles, descriptions, and tags.

03 / Schedule

Publish with a rhythm

Upload to the authorized channel, stagger releases, and review or reprioritize the scheduled queue.
