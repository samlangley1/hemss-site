Privacy policy

hemss / Dmiko / yt-slower

Effective September 27, 2026

1. About this policy

hemss is a locally operated YouTube video preparation and scheduling application, maintained by Sam Langley. The source project is also called Dmiko or yt-slower. This policy covers the application and this informational website. Contact beatsbycia@gmail.com with privacy questions.

The app runs on the operator's computer. This website is not a hosted account service and does not provide a login or receive application credentials.

2. Information the app accesses

  • Public YouTube data: video and channel identifiers, titles, descriptions, publication dates, thumbnails, tags or language information where available, and statistics such as views, likes, and comment counts. Comment counts do not mean comment text is collected.
  • Authorized channel video data: uploaded video identifiers, metadata, privacy status, processing or publication information, and scheduled publication times used to manage the queue.
  • Google authorization: OAuth access and refresh tokens and their related scope and expiry information. Google handles sign-in; the app does not receive or store your Google password.
  • Local work data: selected source URLs, downloaded audio or images, rendered videos, generated metadata, job history, ranking observations, schedules, file references, and operational logs or errors.

With your additional read-only YouTube Analytics authorization, the application collects daily performance reports for your uploads: views, likes, comments, estimated watch time, average viewing duration and percentage, subscriber changes, shares, playlist additions/removals, traffic-source categories, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rates from bulk reports. Reports and timestamped counter observations are stored in the local database and may be exported locally for longitudinal analysis. It does not collect the full YouTube Studio analytics dataset, viewer identities, or your Gmail messages.

3. Why access is needed

The app uses these records to discover and rank candidates, prepare videos, avoid duplicate work, resume interrupted jobs, upload to the channel you authorize, and inspect or update publication schedules and video metadata.

The upload permission (youtube.upload) permits video uploads. Scheduling also requests youtube.force-ssl, a broader YouTube management permission that Google describes as allowing you to see, edit, and permanently delete videos, ratings, comments, and captions. The app uses this authorization for its video and queue operations; requesting the permission does not mean that it collects every category the permission can access.

Analytics collection additionally requests yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly to read your channel performance reports through the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs. It does not request monetary analytics access. With offline authorization, the app stores a refresh token so background runs can obtain new access tokens while your authorization remains valid. You can revoke access at any time.

4. Storage and security

Application records, media, logs, and OAuth tokens are stored locally on the computer running the app. Default locations include credentials/token.json for authorization and data/uploads.sqlite for job records. Paths can vary with configuration. Credentials are excluded from the project's public source repository and from this website.

The app communicates with Google's APIs over HTTPS. Local files are not encrypted by the application itself; their protection depends on the operator's device security, account permissions, disk encryption, and backup practices. Anyone with access to saved tokens may be able to use the associated authorization. Do not share token files or diagnostic logs containing private information.

5. Sharing and external services

The app sends media, titles, descriptions, tags, privacy choices, scheduling instructions, and authenticated API requests to Google/YouTube to perform the requested operations. It retrieves public metadata and source media or artwork from YouTube or other source URLs chosen by the operator. Those services receive the requests and connection information necessary to serve them.

Application data is not sent to a separate hemss-operated cloud backend. It is not sold, supplied to advertising networks or data brokers, used for personalized advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. If you voluntarily send a support message, its contents and your contact details are used to respond to that request; please do not include credentials.

hemss's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This statement is not a claim of Google certification or verification.

Google/YouTube processes information under the Google Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records and authorization remain until the operator removes them or a configured cleanup operation removes eligible files. Media cleanup does not automatically delete the complete database, credentials, or any independently created backups.

  1. Stop scheduled application jobs before removing access or data.
  2. Visit Google Account connections, select hemss, and remove its access to revoke the Google authorization.
  3. Delete the app's local token file, database, logs, downloaded media, and rendered outputs that you no longer want. Remove copies from your backups separately if needed.
  4. Manage or delete videos already uploaded through YouTube Studio. Revoking access or deleting local files does not delete those videos or cancel their schedules.

For help with deletion or any information you sent directly for support, contact beatsbycia@gmail.com. The maintainer cannot remotely delete files on your computer. Google and GitHub handle data they retain under their own policies.

7. This website

This static website uses no application analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, embedded videos, or contact forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request data, including visitors' IP addresses, for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links subjects your visit to those services' policies.

8. Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change, with a revised effective date. Material changes involving new uses of Google user data require appropriate disclosure and consent before those uses begin.

Privacy and support: beatsbycia@gmail.com.
